## Why these parts

- **No side-pressed tact switch is in JLC Basic.** The whole Tactile Switches category
  (14,073 in-stock parts, scanned 2026-11-11) has two Basic parts, both top-pressed: XKB
  TS-1187A-B-A-B (C318884) and XUNPU TS-1088-AR02016 (C720477). TS24CA is the side-pressed
  SMD switch with by far the most stock. Runners-up: HCTL TC-S3601-3.5
  ([C49101620](https://jlcpcb.com/parts/componentSearch?searchTxt=C49101620), 125,949),
  HYP 1TS003B ([C255808](https://jlcpcb.com/parts/componentSearch?searchTxt=C255808), 86,770),
  SHOU HAN TS35CA with a white round plunger, closest in look to Seeed's
  ([C412366](https://jlcpcb.com/parts/componentSearch?searchTxt=C412366), 50,384).
- **MSK12C02** is the side-actuated slide switch with the most stock on JLC.
- **2x12 SMD header** (user's choice: SMD instead of through-hole, male pins): vertical male
  SMD 2x12 2.54 mm headers on JLC, 2026-11-11: HCTL PZ254-2-12-S (1,056, $0.296), hanxia HX
  PZ2.54-2x12P TP ([C42372534](https://jlcpcb.com/parts/componentSearch?searchTxt=C42372534),
  1,031, $0.212, almost no specs listed) and LXWCONN 254PZ-2x12P-SMT-H2.5-PA6.0
  ([C54930269](https://jlcpcb.com/parts/componentSearch?searchTxt=C54930269), 78). HCTL was
  chosen for its full drawing (`PCB/libraries/datasheets/PZ254-2-12-S_C3294470.pdf`): pads
  1.02 x 3.12 mm, 2.26 mm between rows, 8.5 mm overall, plastic 2.5 mm, no locating posts on
  the `-S` part. Its drawing shows no pick-up cap; not yet confirmed that JLC can place it.
  Trade-off: SMD pads take side loads worse than through-hole pins.

BOM after the change: 59 lines, 189 placed parts, 55 distinct LCSC numbers, every line with an
LCSC number (`mindspark_rev1_bom.csv`, regenerated from the schematic with `kicad-cli sch export bom`).
