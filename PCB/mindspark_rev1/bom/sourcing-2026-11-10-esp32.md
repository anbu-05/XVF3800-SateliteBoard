## Changes against the earlier 2026-11-10 check

- Removed: RP2350A (C42411118), AOTA-B201610 inductor (C42411119), ABM8-272-T3 crystal (C20625731), the RP2350's W25Q32 and support passives, SWD 1x3 header (C49257), 1x10 GPIO header (C57369), slave-boot 33R links and the DNP pull-up, the 33R on MIC_CLK.
- Added: ESP32-S3-WROOM-1U-N16R8 ([C3013946](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3013946.html)); one step up from the [WROOM-1U-N8R8](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C2980300.html) because it was cheaper on 2026-11-10 ($5.24 vs $5.84 at LCSC). SN74LVC1G125DBVR mic clock buffer ([C23654](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C23654.html)). 9 × 100R ([C25076](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C25076.html), Basic). 2x10 header ([C56208631](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C56208631.html), 295 in stock). User WS2812B-2020, its SN74AHCT1G125, and EN/BOOT/user buttons (same parts as before).
- Fixed: the mic-supply 10 µF now has an 0603 footprint, matching its part C19702. It was drawn on 0402 before.
- Loose, not assembled: PROG jumper shunt [C100114](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C100114.html); speaker and JTAG lead housings/crimps (see `PCB/libraries/parts-map.json`). Antenna for the IPEX socket: off board, not chosen.
