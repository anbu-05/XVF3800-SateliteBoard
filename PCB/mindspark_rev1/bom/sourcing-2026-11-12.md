## Checks

- **MAX98357AETE+T pins** (ADI datasheet p.15, TQFN column, read in the browser): 1 DIN,
  2 GAIN_SLOT, 3/11/15 GND, 4 SD_MODE, 5/6/12/13 N.C., 7/8 VDD, 9 OUTP, 10 OUTN, 14 LRCLK,
  16 BCLK. The exposed pad "is not internally connected; connect it to a solid ground plane",
  so EP is tied to GND. The stock KiCad `Audio:MAX98357A` symbol has the same numbers.
- **MAX98357AETE+T package** (ADI p.35–37, outline 21-0136, code T1633-4): 3 × 3 × 0.75 mm,
  0.5 mm pitch, leads b 0.20–0.30 mm, L 0.30–0.50 mm, EP 0.95–1.10–1.25 mm. The stock
  `TQFN-16-1EP_3x3mm_P0.5mm_EP1.23x1.23mm` footprint (0.25 × 0.825 mm pads, 1.23 mm EP with
  four paste windows) fits, so its copper is used unchanged.
- **TS5A3166DBVR pins** (TI SCDS188D Pin Functions, DBV column): 1 NO, 2 COM, 3 GND, 4 IN,
  5 V+. Same as the stock `Analog_Switch:TS5A3166DBVR` symbol. Electrical specs are the same
  die as the YZP part (off capacitance 18–19 pF, IN high threshold 2.0 V at 3.3 V).
- Both new packages also clear the open question about 0.4 mm WLP/DSBGA balls against JLC's rules.
