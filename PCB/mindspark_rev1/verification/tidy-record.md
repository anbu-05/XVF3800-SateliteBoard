## Step 1: connection tidying

- **Leads.** Every connected pin has a 100 mil lead to its first bend, junction, anchor or
  connected pin. Exception: R303 (Rf, 24 MHz crystal) has a 600 mil lead to the XOUT node,
  because Rd sits between them.
- **Passive pitch.** Comparable vertical passives sit at 300 mil. A column widens in 50 mil steps
  only where its reference or value text is wider. References are placed above each row and
  values (with rating) below it, outside the gaps.
- **Wired local circuits.** The following are wired directly:
  - both crystals, and the buck SW to inductor paths;
  - the reset button, the RESET pull-up and the flash CS pull-ups;
  - the load-switch ON pull-downs and CT caps;
  - the amp SD_MODE resistor, and the 2N7002 gate and drain parts;
  - the LED series resistors and the WS2812 DOUT to DIN chain;
  - the PLL and mic-supply ferrite nodes, and the TPS2121 divider pairs.
- **Labels kept inside a group.** Each case is listed in `tidy-exceptions.json`:
  - XVF3800 and ESP32 module pins: dense 100 mil pins whose peripherals live in other boxes or sheets.
  - Mic CLK/DATA pins and the mic series-resistor row (one buffered clock fans out to four mics), and ESP_EN/ESP_BOOT between the module, the RC row and the buttons (added 2026-11-10, ESP32 revision).
  - The TPS2121 control pins and the buck FB dividers (FB is 100 mil below SW).
- **Grounds.** Grounds hang down and rails point up from side leads. Where the neighbouring
  pin's lead is in the way, the symbol stays sideways; every such case is in `tidy-exceptions.json`.
- **No floating passives** (revised 2026-11-10 on review). Passives are wired to the pin or port
  they serve: CC Rd at the ESD array's CC pins (staggered), buck FB divider and Cff at FB and the
  inductor output, mic CLK/DATA series resistors at each mic, ESP32 EN RC and GPIO0 pull-up on
  their buttons. PLL_AVDD is now a power symbol, so its ferrite and cap count as a rail filter.
  The TPS2121 symbol was redrawn so its dividers, ILIM, SS and ST pull-up hang off their pins.
- **Wires over labels inside a box** (2026-11-10 on review). Parts in one box are wired, not
  joined by repeated labels: USB D+/D- connector to ESD, JTAG and speaker pads on their lines,
  the mic clock buffer driving one clock wire to all four CLK resistors, speaker amp wired to
  J501, the four flash-switch IN pins on one PRG_EN wire, host-header I2C pull-ups on their
  lines, ESP32 user LED buffer into DIN, and the LED ring's row-1 DOUT round to row-2 DIN.
  Exceptions: USB CC1/CC2 connector-to-ESD (two adjacent Rd shunts make a wired route cross)
  and MCLK_LOOP (opposite sides of U301). `../tools/check_floating.py` checks both rules.
- **Decoupling banks** (revised 2026-11-10 on review). Bypass caps in a box sit in one row;
  adjacent caps on the same rail share one rail wire and one power symbol, and the GND side shares
  one wire and one GND symbol. The flash-switch and mic caps were gathered into one bank each; the
  audio boxes were reordered (mic boxes first, then the amp chain) to stay on A4. Box headings are
  regular weight.
- **Junctions.** Explicit dots wherever three or more wires meet.
  `check_junctions.py` result: PASS, 0 discrepancies.

## Step 2: sheet organisation

- **Paper.** A4 on all nine sheets.
- **Padding.** Measured on KiCad's own SVG plot (`../tools/measure_boxes.py`): every box has
  300 to 348 mil content padding. The extra left/top padding, under 50 mil per edge, is the
  allowance that keeps content on the 50 mil grid. The heading sits inside the top padding.
- **Placement.** Boxes start 100 mil inside the inner border (12 mm, read from the plot) and stay
  at least 100 mil clear of the title block (177, 166 mm). Neighbours are exactly 200 mil apart.
- **Deviation from strict rows.** Each sheet has one tall IC box, so strict rows would not fit
  A4. Boxes are packed bottom-left instead: tops align within a row, and later boxes stack below
  a shorter neighbour, still in reading order. Strict row packing pushed usb_power, xvf3800,
  rp2350 and rp2350_io to A3. You had asked for A4, so A4 was kept. In the ESP32 revision the
  audio sheet's group order (mics, clock buffer, supply, series resistors, amp) was chosen so it
  packs onto A4, the JTAG pads moved 100 mil closer, and the XVF spare-pin pads sit on xvf3800_periph.
- **Overview** (revised 2026-11-10 on review). Each sheet block is fitted to its pins like a
  compacted symbol: width = widest left pin name + 100 mil + widest right pin name (100 mil steps,
  600 mil minimum), height = pin rows at 100 mil plus one 100 mil row above and below. Every block
  sits in its own titled circuit box (heading = the sheet's title) with its stubs, labels and
  name/file fields, and those boxes plus the Mechanical box are packed with the Step 2 padding and
  200 mil gaps. Equal 100 mil labelled stubs; inputs left, outputs/bidirectional right.
